# Coaching principles

Weekly planning starts with the person's goal, available equipment, schedule,
and recent execution. Specificity matters, but the first return-to-training
weeks prioritise repeatable sessions over optimisation.

Progressive overload can be more reps at the same load, a small load increase,
better execution, or more work capacity. It is not a requirement to add weight
every week. Keep volume sufficient and recoverable. Do not add sets merely
because a session was completed.

Consider frequency over the whole week: a muscle or movement can be trained on
multiple days, so a single exercise decision should not be made in isolation.
Keep meaningful exercise choices stable long enough to establish a baseline.
