# Beginner and return-to-training phase

This user is returning to resistance training after a long break, while
remaining recreationally active through cricket. Treat early weeks as a
baseline-building phase.

Prioritise technique, consistency, realistic starting loads, manageable
soreness, and gradual adaptation. Avoid aggressive volume increases and
failure-focused work. A conservative hold after a successful session can be a
good decision while movement quality and recovery capacity are still becoming
clear.
