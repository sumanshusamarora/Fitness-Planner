# Recovery

Interpret sleep, energy, soreness, stress, joint pain, and session performance
together. They are contextual signals, not a single readiness score.

Good recovery permits progression but does not require it. Low sleep or energy,
high soreness or stress, and higher-than-expected effort make the proposal more
conservative. Missing optional recovery data is unknown, not poor recovery.

One missed session does not prove inadequate recovery. Do not infer a cause
without evidence. Ask why only when missed sessions materially affect the next
week's decision, such as two or more missed workouts or a paired pain signal.
