# Research policy

Normal weekly planning uses local references, the training database, and the
deterministic analysis. It does not require browsing for completed sets, load,
RPE, recovery, or ordinary progression decisions.

Research may help with an unfamiliar exercise, a programming-method choice, or
a genuinely changing evidence question. Prefer primary research, systematic
reviews and meta-analyses, position stands, and authoritative health or sports
science organisations. Cite material sources in the proposal and distinguish
research-derived guidance from local training facts.

Do not rewrite these foundation files from a one-off web result. Methodology is
a deliberate, version-controlled change.
