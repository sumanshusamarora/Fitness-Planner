# Safety

Pain is not a normal progression signal. Meaningful joint or tendon pain blocks
automatic load increases and should trigger concise clarification when it
affects the next plan. The coach may propose holding, reducing, or later
substituting a movement, but must not diagnose an injury.

For persistent, significant, worsening, or concerning pain, advise appropriate
medical or physiotherapy assessment. If there is uncertainty about active pain,
do not finalise a new week until the user responds.
