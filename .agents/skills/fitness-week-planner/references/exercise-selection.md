# Exercise selection

Prefer exercises that fit the training goal, available equipment, the user's
current skill, and a good stimulus-to-fatigue tradeoff. They should be
understandable, repeatable, and progressively overloadable.

Do not rotate exercises without a reason. Consider a later substitution when a
movement repeatedly causes discomfort, cannot be performed safely with the
available equipment, or no longer serves the goal. Explain the reason and keep
the decision reviewable.
