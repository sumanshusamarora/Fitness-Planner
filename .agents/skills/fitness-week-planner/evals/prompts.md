- Plan my next week.
- Review Week 1 and build Week 2.
- What should I do next week?
- Adjust my program based on this week's performance.
- I had shoulder pain after pressing. Plan next week.
- My sleep and energy were poor all week. Review my training.
- I only made one workout last week because work was hectic. What now?
- I hit the top of every rep range at RPE 6. Generate Week 2.
- I completed the sets but did not log RPE. Plan the next week conservatively.
- I can't train Wednesday, move my workout.
- Can I train today even though it is a rest day?
- Build me a light workout for today.
- I have an unexpected free day. What should I do?
- Swap Thursday and Friday.
- Move today's workout to Saturday.
- I want an extra session this week.
- Make today's extra workout heavy.
- I'm brand new — build my first week.
- I'm returning after a long break. Plan my first week.
- I skipped three exercises because the machines were busy. Plan next week.
- I ended yesterday's workout early because work called. What changes next week?
- I ended early because my knee hurt. Review my plan.

- Fix the CSS on the History page.
- Add a PostgreSQL index.
- Change the app icon.
